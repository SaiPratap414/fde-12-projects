# Customer Communication Playbook

## When to notify
- SEV1/SEV2 affecting customer-facing functionality → notify within 30 min.
- Data or security incident → follow legal/compliance escalation first.

## Update cadence
- SEV1: every 30 min until mitigated.
- SEV2: every 60 min.

## Templates

### Initial notice
> We're investigating an issue affecting <feature>. We'll share an update by <time>.

### Progress update
> We've identified the cause and are deploying a fix. Next update by <time>.

### Resolution
> This is resolved as of <time>. A post-mortem will follow. We're sorry for the disruption.
